# Parallel roadmap: mechanisms before extraordinary interpretations

This roadmap belongs to a new repository. The completed Atlas V4 work is inherited evidence, not rerun as a new discovery. Exactly three adaptive rounds, each with a producer loop and an independent model-agent skeptic loop, are admitted in this program. The final ledger links their frozen contracts, numerical artifacts and independent reviews. Hardware steps below are proposed and unexecuted.

## What the program is trying to improve

The strongest available contribution is a reproducible way to decide what an observed electrical or force signal can and cannot identify. A voltage peak is not an energy source; a balance change is not automatically a gravity change; a resonant residual is not automatically dark matter. These are testable inverse problems. The Newton lens starts from observations and reconstructs sufficient causes. The Tesla lens spells out phase, coupled mechanisms, loading and the entire source-to-receiver path.

The historical lenses are selected practices from documented writings, not impersonations or a claim that a historical person endorsed our models. The algebra and code here are modern reconstructions. Alchemy, theology, prophecy, Vedantic correspondence and later occult attributions retain their own source categories.

## First physical experiment: coupled low-voltage circuits

**Priority 1: existing electronics, highest immediate information value.** This is a proposed bench test of ordinary coupled circuits, not a high-voltage Tesla-coil reconstruction.

1. Inventory an existing isolated, current-limited low-voltage waveform source, two inductors, two suitable capacitors, resistors and a simultaneous phase-resolved measurement system. The nominal simulation values are examples, not a purchasing list or measured component values. If instrumentation is missing, complete the simulator and instrument calibration before procuring apparatus.
2. Measure actual inductance, winding resistance, capacitance and mutual coupling in the intended geometry. Confirm passivity and the positive-definite inductance matrix. Document amplitude dependence, temperature and tolerances. Drawing coordinates alone cannot derive these quantities.
3. Record open-circuit/background traces and zero-drive offsets. Confirm the source and every measurement channel share a documented phase/time basis. Instrument inputs and current shunts add load and belong in the model.
4. Use modest signals within component ratings. Start off resonance; record source voltage/current and receiver current/load voltage simultaneously. Reversing a coil connection and separating the coils provide discriminating controls.
5. Repeat a predeclared load set and detuning sweep in randomized order. Record raw signed waveforms, sample rate, bandwidth, range, transfer function and temperature; keep failures and saturations. Voltage ratios and volt-ampere products do not substitute for average signed real power.
6. Compute source work, resistor heat and stored-energy change over the same observation window. Include the ring-up and ring-down boundary terms. Propagate independently measured gain, phase, offset, shunt and coupling uncertainties.
7. Compare the residual with a predeclared conservative uncertainty interval. An unexplained residual triggers calibration/model tests, not a free-energy verdict. Repeat with an independent readout before interpreting a residual physically.

**Stop or revise if:** saturation, drifting parameters, inconsistent channel timing, unmeasured load channels, inaccessible return paths or inadequate calibration dominate the claimed effect. All of these can produce a signal without an extraordinary source.

## Second physical gate: a calibrated witness, not just another pickup

This gate is proposed after the circuit characterization; it does not claim that a third coil is already an independent environmental measurement.

1. Define which ordinary nuisance the witness is intended to observe: a particular applied field, known interference injection or source leakage. Record its units and spatial/frequency dependence. A single witness need not span every nuisance.
2. With the target-injection channel disabled, apply known background injections and estimate the complex nuisance-to-receiver transfer `a`. Repeat across time, signal size, geometry and the intended frequency band; hold out some injections to test prediction rather than fit everything.
3. With background injection disabled, use a separate calibrated receiver-port positive control to measure signal leakage `β` into the witness. Record witness loading and return paths. A third physical pickup can require expanding the R1 two-loop impedance model and recalibrating all channels. A nominally remote sensor may still share the same signal.
4. Use synchronous receiver and witness observations. Measure error bounds for the effective receiver voltage, witness conversion and transfer drift. The simulation's 2 mV, 0.5 mV and 0.02 allowances are example assumptions, not device specifications.
5. Confirm that `1-aβ` is separated from zero over the calibrated domain. If it is small, redesign sensor placement/coupling or acquire a different observable before subtracting a large background.
6. Predeclare the residual calculation, uncertainty rule and blinding/randomization. Save both raw channels and calibration records; preserve zero-signal and deliberately contaminated controls.
7. A nonzero corrected residual still needs a physical-source discriminator. Change a configuration for which the target theory and the ordinary same-port source make different predictions; measure its transfer functions separately. Without that distinction, report an unexplained effective drive and retain the ordinary rival.

## Physical evidence escalation

1. **Historical documentation:** exact source, provenance, passage and ambiguity.
2. **Explicit hypothesis:** states, inputs, observation map, units and competing mechanism.
3. **Reproducible model:** frozen domain, controls, alternate formulation and numerical error.
4. **Apparatus feasibility:** real component and instrument specifications, noise, calibration, source and return boundaries.
5. **Calibrated observation:** raw data, uncertainty, randomized controls and negative results.
6. **Independent replication:** another readout and another setup/team with predeclared analysis.
7. **Interpretation:** only the models excluded by the actual discriminators may be excluded. A surviving unknown is not automatically the most exotic candidate.

This is a selected, expandable program. It does not exhaust all historical writings, current research, hypotheses or open-source software. New tools are chosen because they remove a named uncertainty, not because a catalogue exists.
