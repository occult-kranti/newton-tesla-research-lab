# A new Newton–Tesla research program

This repository answers the user's separate request for three adaptive rounds with two loops per round. It follows the earlier five-round Atlas continuation, whose results remain in that repository. It does not relabel the earlier work as new research.

## Research method

Newton's documented analysis/synthesis and hidden-cause practices and Tesla's mechanism, phase, resonance and whole-device accounting are methodological sources. They do not recreate either historical person's mind or imply endorsement. The advisor, producer, skeptic, source researcher, interface designer and apparatus illustrator are contemporary model agents with correlated model-family authorship, not human peer reviewers. Model defaults are inherited; no external provider or unreported model comparison is claimed.

Each production loop freezes its question, assumptions, units, controls and numerical acceptance criteria before implementation. A separate skeptical loop executes independent calculations and challenges the interpretation. The next contract follows the actual preceding verdict. Source exploration, UI creation, drawing and publication are parallel workstreams, not additional research loops.

## Breadth and evidence

The grouped research catalogue covers electrical energy and magnetic mechanisms, gravity and dark-matter inference, historical chymistry, symbolic interpretations and Newton's theological predictions. Selected historical books, manuscripts, patents, government reports and modern papers are recorded with actual reading depth. This is an expandable selection, not a claim to have read all books, all current research or every possible hypothesis.

Historical assertion, attributed transcription, patent proposal, modern scientific result, mathematical identity, executed simulation, proposed setup and measured apparatus result remain distinct. No new physical measurements are supplied by this project. A patent grant, government custody, historical authority, visual pattern or simulation does not establish free energy, modified gravity, dark matter or supernatural influence.

The detailed catalogue can retain an imaginative idea without pretending it has a mechanism. An executable hypothesis needs an observable, a competing explanation and a result that would distinguish them. A diagram supplies geometry and a measurement boundary; it does not certify sensitivity, hardware calibration or an operated experiment.
