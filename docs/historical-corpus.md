# Historical corpus and modern reconstructions

This is a selected-passage dossier, not a claim to have read all of Newton or Tesla. It distinguishes an author's recorded experiment, a proposal, an interpretation and a present-day model. URLs and exact reading depths resolve through [sources.json](../data/sources.json). None of the historical material substitutes for a calibrated new observation.

## Newton: striking work with distinct kinds of prediction

| Source and passage read | Documented idea | Useful modern reconstruction | What cannot be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [1672 letter on light and colours](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00006), printed pages 3076–3078; `NT-NEWTON-LIGHT1672` | Newton varied prism/glass geometry, restricted rays using apertures and compared their behavior at a second prism. His experimental argument distinguished different refrangibility from several geometric alternatives. | A ray trace with declared indices and aperture geometry; compare a wavelength-dependent model with a single-index rival. Reverse the second prism and test geometric limiting cases. | A modern ray diagram is not a recovered photograph, measured historical spectrum or exact reproduction of his dimensions. |
| [De motu corporum in gyrum](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00089), Theorem 2 corollary 5 and Theorem 3; `NT-NEWTON-MOTION` | Period/radius relations constrain centripetal force scaling. | For circular motion, combine acceleration `4π²r/T²` with `T² ∝ r³` to recover an inverse-square scaling; then test forward trajectories. | A circular reconstruction alone does not prove a universal many-body law, establish dark matter or identify the force carrier. |
| [Opticks, 1718, Query 31](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00051), final printed pages 380–382; `NT-NEWTON-ANALYSIS` | Analysis proceeds from observations toward causes; synthesis tests whether the proposed causes explain phenomena, with qualifications on inductive reach. | State measurements, a candidate mechanism, a competing mechanism and a prediction not used to fit the mechanism. | This method is not authority for a specific modern exotic theory. |
| [Chymical notebook](https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/text/ALCH00109/normalized), opening December 1678 entries, ff. 1r–1v; `NT-NEWTON-CHYMISTRY` | Quantities, residues and failed or altered mixtures were recorded alongside historical names and symbols. | A text-based substance-candidate table with unresolved mixtures; ask which independent composition evidence would distinguish candidates. | Color, a name or a residue does not establish elemental transmutation. No mixture was prepared here. |
| [Emerald Tablet transcription](https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/text/ALCH00017/normalized), catalogue and English text ff. 2r–2v; `NT-NEWTON-HERMES` | Newton copied/translated an older Hermetic text using correspondences and transformations. | If a correspondence inspires a model, define its states, units, observable map and failure conditions. | Copying a text is not authorship of every claim or proof of a field theory. The Latin commentary was not translated in this review. |

These distinctions let unusual historical questions remain investigable. A resemblance can suggest a calculation; it cannot supply missing measurements.

## The 2060 manuscript, without the apocalypse myth

Two primary scholarly transcriptions were directly read:

1. [Yahuda MS 7.3g, f. 13v, normalized transcription THEM00437](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/THEM00437) discusses 1,260 prophetic days interpreted as years and an 800 CE starting point, producing 2060. Newton qualifies the endpoint: **“It may end later, but I see no reason for its ending sooner.”** He then explains that his purpose is to restrain rash conjectures rather than assert an exact end time.
2. [Yahuda MS 7.3o, f. 8r, diplomatic transcription THEM00430](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/THEM00430) gives conditional chronology bounds. Its upper endpoint after the phrase about 2060 is blank in the transcription. This review does not fill that blank using later commentary or arithmetic. The adjacent addressed slip is relevant to dating discussions, but the whole manuscript collection's dates do not automatically date each fragment.

The source record `NT-NEWTON-PROPHECY` links these actual manuscripts and identifies the separate scholarly contextual discussion. Manuscript images were not independently collated. We do not call the passage a demonstrated successful prediction, a physical law or a countdown. The modest testable exercise is a sensitivity table: varying an assumed chronology start shifts its derived endpoint. That examines the calculation's premises and does not adjudicate theology.

## Tesla: impressive apparatus, traceable sources of energy

**The 1892 lecture (`TESLA-1892`).** Selected passages around figures 2 and 3 in the [lecture transcription](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/13476/pg13476-images.html) describe a generator, a charging condenser and induction coils. Tesla discusses terminal capacity, screens, losses and the way nearby objects alter behavior. These make coupled circuits and environmental pickup appropriate modern subjects. Dramatic discharge appearance is not a measurement of net work. Our low-voltage/synthetic coil reconstruction does not reproduce the high-voltage historical apparatus.

**Earth/atmosphere transmission (`NT-TESLA-TRANSMISSION`).** [US645576A, 20 March 1900](https://patents.google.com/patent/US645576A/en) describes a driven transmitting system and tuned reception, including earth/air conduction claims. The inspected source includes a generator and condenser. Claimed transmission performance is a patent assertion here, not a independently reproduced efficiency result. The local project models coupled coils; it does not implement earth-scale propagation.

**Radiant-energy collection (`NT-TESLA-RADIANT`).** [US685957A, 5 November 1901](https://patents.google.com/patent/US685957A/en) proposes charging a condenser from radiation and arranging its discharge. Some arrangements include additional powered apparatus. The useful low-material analogue is a specified external input, storage and load with a complete energy budget. It is not proof of output without an input. No radioactive, X-ray or high-voltage recreation is proposed.

Remote-control boats, Colorado Springs notebooks, mechanical oscillators and later popular “death ray” stories are not primary-text investigations in this bounded corpus. They are not quietly treated as validated results or as books read. The documented transmission and radiant-energy proposals already provide unusual, testable questions without importing those additional stories.

## Alchemical words to modern candidates

The [Chymistry of Isaac Newton glossary](https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/page/glossary), selected entries, supplies the contextual reference (`NT-CHYMISTRY-GLOSSARY`). The table is a candidate dictionary. Element symbols do not establish purity, and an early-modern substance name often spans mixtures, phases or symbolic uses.

| Historical term or symbol | Modern candidate in a literal material context | Ambiguity that must remain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sol / Sun | Gold, `Au` | A symbolic or process-specific use need not denote a pure gold sample. |
| Luna / Moon | Silver, `Ag` | Selected disguised uses can refer to antimony-related material; context overrides universal substitution. |
| Mars | Iron, `Fe` | Some disguised uses concern an antimony product or its preparation context. |
| Venus | Copper, `Cu` | Some contextual uses designate silver; the name alone is insufficient. |
| Saturn | Lead, `Pb` | Some chymical contexts use it for antimony ore/material rather than metallic lead. |
| Mercury / quicksilver | Elemental mercury, `Hg`, in literal use | “Sophic” mercury is not securely a single modern element or formula. |
| Stibium / antimony ore | Stibnite, `Sb₂S₃`, as a leading mineral candidate | Historical ore may include impurities and other phases; it is not pure antimony metal by definition. |
| Regulus of antimony | Antimony-rich metallic product, often represented by `Sb` | Composition can include other metals; no universal alloy formula follows. |
| Vermilion | Mercury sulfide, `HgS` | Color does not establish phase purity or exclude mixtures. |
| Vitriol | Metal sulfate, often iron or copper sulfate | Cation, hydration and mixed composition require context; “vitriol” is not one formula. |
| Salt of tartar | Potassium carbonate, `K₂CO₃`, as a principal candidate | Historical product composition and water content are not certified. |
| Sal ammoniac | Ammonium chloride, `NH₄Cl`, as a principal candidate | Historical purity and admixtures remain unresolved. |

The discriminating evidence would be provenance plus independent elemental and phase analysis, not a matching color or a planetary symbol. This project supplies no assay of historical samples and no recipes for hazardous chymical materials. It treats the dictionary as a source-bound inference problem, not decipherment of a secret universal periodic table.

## Scope of the occult and astrology branch

Hermetic symbolism, prophetic chronology and astrological rule systems belong to different historical traditions and are not merged into a single physical mechanism. The linked astrology workbench can support historical study and geometry. Its numerical and empirical validation boundaries are documented in [tool-audit.md](tool-audit.md). A future predictive test would need independently fixed outcomes, blinded scoring and a baseline; this project has performed no such human-outcome study.
