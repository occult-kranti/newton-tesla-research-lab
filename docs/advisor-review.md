## Novelty and missing premises

The contribution claimed here is a project-specific, reproducible experimental-design package: frozen synthetic fixtures, independent counterchecks, explicit indistinguishable rivals and diagrams whose measurement boundaries match the algebra. Coupled-circuit passivity, inverse-map rank and bounded-error propagation are established methods. This project does not claim priority for a new physical law or an exhaustive literature comparison.

The next missing premise is physical calibration, not another attractive rendering. Actual inductances, mutual coupling, instrument phase/gain/noise, transfer stability, source and return channels must be measured before numerical residuals can be used as experimental evidence. Even a calibrated unexplained receiver drive would require a distinguishing physical model and independent replication before being assigned to dark matter, gravity modification or any other extraordinary cause.
