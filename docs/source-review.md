## What this review supports

The best first experiments use a passive two-coil model, independent power accounting and controlled injected signals. These need only computation initially. They connect to Tesla's documented apparatus while exposing the assumptions that can make resonance look like energy creation. Modern magnetic dark-matter receivers add a useful lesson: a sensitive resonator still requires end-to-end calibration, a specified signal model and environmental controls. These connections are methodological, not evidence that Tesla detected dark matter.

## Current academic evidence

**ABRACADABRA, 2021 (`NT-ABRA2021`).** Selected methods, results and Supplement III were read in the [primary article](https://arxiv.org/html/2102.06722v1). Its calibration loop injects a known flux with a declared geometrical relationship to the hypothesized signal. The full chain includes current, mutual inductance, flux and readout gain. Magnet-off comparisons, narrow moving spectral features, mains-related lines and mechanical motion in fringe fields show why a peak alone is insufficient. An uncalibrated run informed analysis rather than reported exclusion bounds. This is a measured search in that apparatus and parameter range; the present project has neither replicated it nor acquired particle-search data.

**DMRadio-Core, 2026 (`NT-DMRADIO-CORE2026`).** Selected sections II, III.1 and IV.1 of the [design preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.16602v1) describe a segmented solenoid and external LC pickup, modeled geometry and projected sensitivity. These are design calculations, not a detection. Two source issues are retained rather than silently repaired: the version header reports 17 April 2026 while the rendered body displays 24 August 2026; equation 2's cosine appears to use a cyclic-frequency expression without the corresponding angular-frequency factor, whereas section III.1 explicitly defines the cyclic frequency. Our circuit work does not import that cosmological equation.

**SQUID noise matching (`NT-SQUID-NOISE2025`).** Selected section III equations of the [primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.20398v1) distinguish signal transfer, loading, imprecision and backaction. The submission record is 29 April 2025; the rendered body date is 24 August 2026. We retain both and do not infer a new version from the body date. Its specific detector assumptions cannot be collapsed into a claim that maximizing voltage maximizes discovery sensitivity.

**Forces and levitation, 2024.** The inspected [steady electromagnetic field tests](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.15640) use shielded balances and controls for magnetic interactions, thermal drift and vacuum behavior (`NT-TAJMAR2024`). Their bounded null findings are not a theorem excluding every conceivable effect. The [diamagnetic graphite study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11071-024-10018-x) concerns ordinary force balance, stiffness and damping (`NT-GRAPHITE2024`). Its precision apparatus is not reproduced by a simple illustrative magnet model.

## Government records, patents and unusual claims

The NASA [2004 asymmetric-capacitor report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20040171929/downloads/20040171929.pdf) is an actual technical investigation. Its air observations are consistent with momentum transfer to gas; coarse vacuum sensitivity and discharge events do not establish steady vacuum propulsion. The NASA [2005 Breakthrough Propulsion Physics assessment](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20060000022/downloads/20060000022.pdf) separates readiness, reliability and immediacy. Neither record is current government confirmation of an exotic propulsion system.

Three modern patents are retained because they state mechanisms that can be challenged:

- [US11511891B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11511891B2/en) proposes electrostatic propulsion. Its claims do not supply independent confirmation, and the inspected text does not make it a NASA-sponsored validation.
- [US10144532B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10144532B2/en) is Navy-assigned and proposes resonant electromagnetic cavity effects and inertial-mass reduction. Assignment establishes legal provenance; it does not establish the proposed effect.
- [US12553420B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12553420B2/en), granted **17 February 2026**, proposes propulsion using cyclic internal moving masses. Selected abstract, claims and description were read. A complete center-of-mass simulation is the inexpensive discriminating next step; housing movement on a support is not isolated-system translation. Citation of another patent is not validation of either one.

These are proposals worth translating into explicit boundaries and observables. None was promoted to an established source of free energy or antigravity. Tesla's original transmission and radiant-energy patents are treated by the same rule.
